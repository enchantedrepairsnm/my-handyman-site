New Mexico Handyman Scope & Compliance

Transparency & Licensing Boundaries

Enchanted Repairs operates in full alignment with New Mexico Regulation and Licensing Department (RLD) minor work guidelines.

Permitted Handyman Work:

  • Interior mounting (TVs, curtain rods, shelves, pictures)
  • Minor wall repairs, patching, touch-up painting
  • Fence picket/gate repairs and wood soffit installation
  • Replacing existing light fixtures, outlets, or faucets (like-for-like)
  • Furniture assembly and minor woodwork updates

Licensed Contractor Boundaries:

  • New main electrical panel wiring or circuit breaker installs
  • Structural beam modifications or major load-bearing framing
  • Main water/gas line re-piping or underground utility work
